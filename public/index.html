Procedures

How Document Requesting Works

Our streamlined 4-step workflow ensures transparency and speed.

1

Submit Request

Log in to your Student Portal, select your document, and state your purpose of request.

2

Registrar Review

The Registrar Office verifies your student status, clearance, and enrollment validity.

3

Processing & Seal

The document is prepared, signed by college authorities, and dry-sealed with the CCA official seal.

4

Claim & Release

Once marked "Completed", present your Student ID at the Registrar Window to claim your document.

Student Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track the status of my request?

Log in to the Student Portal and open My Requests & Tracking. Your submissions display a live 3-step progress stepper.

Can I request multiple documents?

Yes. You can submit separate requests for each document type as required. All requests will remain tracked under your Student ID.

What does "Processing" status indicate?

"Processing" indicates that the Registrar has approved your submission and is preparing, validating, and sealing your document.

Where do I claim my completed document?

Visit the Office of the Registrar, Christ Centered Academy of Lipa City Inc.
