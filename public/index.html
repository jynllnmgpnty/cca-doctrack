Procedure Guide

How Document Requesting Works

Our streamlined 4-step process ensures transparency and speedy release.

1

Submit Request

Access the Student Portal, fill in your student ID, select the document type, and specify your purpose.

2

Registrar Review

The College Registrar verifies your student record, clearance status, and enrollment validity.

3

Processing & Seal

Credentials are generated, signed by designated college officials, and stamped with the official college seal.

4

Claim & Release

Once marked "Completed", present your CCA Student ID at the Registrar Window to claim your documents.

Help & Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check the real-time status of my request?

Enter the Student Portal, click My Requests & Tracking, and your submitted requests will display with a visual 3-step progress bar.

Can I request more than one document?

Yes. You may submit separate requests for each document type as needed. All submissions will be organized under your student history.

What does "Processing" status mean?

"Processing" means the registrar has accepted your request and is currently printing, authenticating, and signing your document.

Where do I claim my completed document?

Claim your document at the Office of the College Registrar, Ground Floor, Admin Building, City College of Angeles.
